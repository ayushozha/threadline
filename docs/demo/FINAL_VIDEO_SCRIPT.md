# Threadline — “Tell me about your sunflower”

## Project and pitch

**Threadline — A little closer. Every day.**

Threadline helps older adults and people with disabilities take part in everyday life and stay connected to family. Wearable audio, visual context and Gemini bring natural, personalized assistance into the moment.

## Two-minute story

Evelyn is a grandmother with low vision. Emma is her daughter; Lily is her
granddaughter. Low vision makes small print inaccessible; it does not imply that
Evelyn has memory loss. She decides what to ask, what to say and what to share.
Use Jessica for Emma's narration. Give the spoken dialogue room to breathe.

## Production notes

- Dialogue is rehearsal copy. Use the actual Gemini response in a product demo;
  do not replace a failed response with scripted audio and call it live.
- Have a real card with the exact legible sunflower sentence, and a saved,
  consented statement about the roses. The model reads the card; it does not
  identify Lily from a face or guess what hidden handwriting says.
- The family starts the call. Threadline currently prepares drafts and opens a
  messaging app; it does not independently place this call or verify delivery.
- Briefly label illustrative footage/inputs as examples. Device recognition and
  a recorded demo do not establish reliable assistance in all environments.
- Use the three available clips only where their contents match the story.
  Additional MiniMax footage can bridge human moments, with its origin marked.
- Cut any explanation before cutting the pauses in Evelyn and Lily's exchange.
  The payoff is their conversation, not a sequence of product screens.
