[warmly] Memory loss can interrupt an ordinary morning. The difficult moment is not always forgetting a name. Sometimes, it is forgetting what comes next.

[storytelling] In this fictional example, Meera planned to make tea, then water her plant. A phone call interrupted her. Now she asks, “What was I doing?”

Threadline connects her saved words with the current camera view. The blue mug is still empty. Her earlier conversation supplies the missing plan.

The camera alone cannot know her intention. A transcript alone cannot show what is in front of her now.

The agent can say, “You were making tea. Let’s finish that first,” and show the evidence behind it. It can also recall where she left her glasses, and draft a caregiver note for her approval.

This is a prototype for everyday memory support. It does not diagnose dementia or confirm task completion.

[reassuring] Less searching. One clear next step. More confidence to carry on.
